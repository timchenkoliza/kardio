Architecture comparison

Три разных ответа на один и тот же продукт.

Одинаковый content set, одинаковые product boundaries и одинаковая задача: дать кардиологу быстрый доступ к trusted professional knowledge и инструментам без patient/EHR контекста.

Concept A

Search-first Navigator

«Я знаю, что мне нужно → KARDIO быстро ведёт к ответу, инструменту или первоисточнику».

  • dominant search
  • 4 quick jobs
  • recent / pinned
  • compact update
  • source-first results

Strength: fastest first value.

Risk: may feel like a specialised search engine.

Concept B

Professional Toolbelt

«Мои основные professional capabilities всегда рядом; поиск — универсальный fallback».

  • persistent capability rail
  • central active workspace
  • provenance side panel
  • explicit repeat-use model
  • no hidden personalization

Strength: repeat utility + strong product identity.

Risk: freezes taxonomy too early.

Открыть B
Concept C

Knowledge Workspace

«Профессиональные темы и источники живут в устойчивом рабочем пространстве; поиск встроен в него».

  • topic/collection navigation
  • embedded search
  • source stack
  • explicit user ownership
  • natural later Gen-UI path

Strength: strongest long-term architecture.

Risk: setup/complexity before proven utility.

Selection gate

  1. First value is obvious in the first screen.
  2. Source/provenance is visible without clutter.
  3. Four core jobs are reachable without ambiguity.
  4. Repeat utility improves without inferred personalization.
  5. Phone layout is native, not collapsed desktop.
  6. Architecture is feasible for a 3.5-person team.
  7. Later Gen-UI can be added without making AI the primary UI.

Current hypothesis

A is the strongest MVP baseline. B is the strongest challenger. C is the most promising long-term workspace model but the riskiest MVP starting point.

The prototype review should test whether B or C produces enough repeat-use advantage to justify additional complexity versus A.

These scores are design hypotheses, not user-research results. No concept is selected until review. Medical claims, production provider selection and source trust are intentionally not invented.
