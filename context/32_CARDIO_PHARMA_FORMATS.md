# Cardiology pharma partner formats v1

Scope: cardiology only. This document is a product-format reference, not legal approval and not a source of therapeutic claims.

## Reference patterns

### Moderna HCP pattern

Use the structural idea, not the trade dress or copy:

- lead with one concrete workflow friction or HCP job
- keep the product proposition singular and operational
- place prescribing / safety information close to the claim
- provide self-service HCP utilities: quick-reference guide, handling / administration support, product information
- separate patient-facing and HCP-facing destinations

Product implication for us: a sponsored card can be useful before it is promotional. The strongest version behaves like a compact HCP tool with explicit provenance and brand disclosure.

### Bayer HCP pattern

- HCP gate before promotional content
- disease-area hubs
- podcasts, webinars, events, product resources and HCP tools in one professional environment
- evidence / education and product content are distinct surfaces

Product implication for us: partner inventory should not be one banner format. It should include learning, events, tools and product-resource routes.

### Gedeon Richter HCP pattern

- specialty-specific professional portal
- expert interviews and webinars
- scientific-study reviews
- treatment algorithms and clinical cases
- online meetings selected by topic and time
- visible HCP-only disclosure and pharmacovigilance route

Product implication for us: sponsored clinical content can be a case, expert session, evidence review or meeting invitation, provided the sponsor relationship is explicit.

### Geropharm professional-engagement pattern

Public company materials describe a mixed digital / professional-engagement stack: websites, social channels, webinars, email, video, expert boards, conferences, masterclasses and professional competitions.

Product implication for us: support both always-on native placements and event / expert-session formats instead of reducing pharma to display advertising.

### AstraZeneca CVRM pattern

- disease-area communication built around interconnected cardiovascular, renal and metabolic risk
- clinical-system initiatives, early detection, screening and pathway change
- collaboration with HCPs and professional organisations

Product implication for us: unbranded disease education and pathway-improvement formats should exist alongside brand/product formats.

### PROMOMED

Use as a company / portfolio reference only until a specific public HCP advertising execution is selected and documented. Do not infer a format from corporate positioning alone.

## Prototype inventory in Kardio

All company and product names rendered in the product are synthetic.

1. `RX product utility` — one product job + instruction / safety route
2. `RX case → evidence` — case first, product information secondary and explicitly sponsored
3. `HCP tool` — quick guide / practical professional utility
4. `Unbranded disease education` — sponsor-supported topic without product branding
5. `OTC → HCP` — professional education around an OTC scenario with the commercial layer visibly separated

## Separation rules

- always label the block as partner / sponsored content
- never mix sponsored ranking with editorial ranking without a visible disclosure
- sponsor and synthetic brand remain visible on the card
- partner cards never inherit `verified`, `recommended`, `best`, `preferred` or similar editorial trust language
- no generated efficacy, safety, dosing, indication or comparative superiority claim
- therapeutic claims can enter only from an approved source and legal / medical review
- pharmacovigilance / safety route belongs to the partner destination contract

## Russia legal gate

For the current prototype, RX cards are demonstration mechanics only.

Before any real commercial release, channel eligibility must be reviewed separately. Russian advertising law and FAS guidance impose specific restrictions on prescription-drug advertising and the permitted channels. Do not infer that a verified-doctor online product automatically qualifies as a permitted RX advertising channel.

OTC and disease-awareness formats also require content / disclaimer review before launch.
