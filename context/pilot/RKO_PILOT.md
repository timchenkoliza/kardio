# RKO Pilot Proposal

## Goal

Validate whether a modern distribution/search layer around authoritative cardiology knowledge creates repeat professional utility.

Do not ask RKO to outsource medical authority to the technology company.

Ask RKO to become:
- scientific/content partner;
- source authority;
- expert network;
- distribution partner.

## What the technology company brings

- product/UX;
- web/PWA;
- search;
- source navigation;
- content structuring;
- analytics;
- experiments;
- fast iteration.

## What to ask from RKO

- 1 scientific owner;
- access to 2–4 expert reviewers;
- agreement on which materials may be indexed/summarized/adapted;
- review rules;
- co-branded pilot distribution;
- structured feedback from participating cardiologists.

## Pilot scope

### Cohort A — discovery alpha
20–30 cardiologists.
4 weeks.

Purpose:
- find top queries;
- verify usability;
- inspect failure cases.

### Cohort B — RKO pilot
100–300 verified physicians if distribution permits.
6–8 weeks.

Purpose:
- repeat utility;
- source trust;
- retrieval success;
- specialty-source behavior.

## Product included

- guideline search with citations;
- calculators;
- drug/reference entry points;
- 10 core topics;
- 20–40 practical scenarios;
- selected RKO updates;
- saves/recent;
- explicit feedback.

## Excluded

- patient data;
- diagnosis;
- treatment recommendation for a real patient;
- EHR integrations;
- pharma ads;
- algorithmic personalization in first pilot.

## Review workflow

```text
source → AI/editorial draft → clinical review → publish → usage feedback → correction log
```

Every published medical artifact gets:
- source;
- reviewer;
- version/date;
- status;
- correction history.

## Partnership pitch

"Вы сохраняете медицинскую авторитетность и редакционный контроль. Мы даём современный search/UX/distribution layer и измеряем, какие профессиональные задачи врач реально закрывает быстрее."
